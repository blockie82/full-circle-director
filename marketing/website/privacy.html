Privacy Policy

Full Circle Director and Full Circle Player · Last updated 7 November 2026

In short: Full Circle Director doesn't collect, send or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Everything it handles stays on your own PC, your own headsets and your own local network, and Full Circle Immersive never receives any of it.

Who we are

Full Circle Director (the control panel that runs on a Windows PC) and Full Circle Player (the page that opens on each VR headset) are made by Full Circle Immersive, based in the United Kingdom. This policy explains what information the software handles and where it goes.

What the software handles, and where it stays

To play video in sync across headsets, the software handles some information on your local network. None of it leaves your network, and none of it is sent to us.

InformationWhyWhere it's kept
Headset names you choose, and a random ID for each headsetTo list and label headsets in the control panelOn the PC, in Documents\Full Circle Director, and in each headset's browser
Each headset's local network addressSo the PC and headsets can talk to each otherOnly while connected; not saved
Battery level, free storage and playback statusShown on the control panel so the person running the session can see each headset is readyOnly while connected; not saved
The direction each headset is facingShown on the control panel's preview so the session leader can see where people are lookingOnly while connected; not saved or recorded
Your videos and images, and their file namesTo play them on the headsetsOn the PC, and copied to each headset's browser storage when you send them
A security certificateCreated on your PC so headsets can connect securely over your networkOn the PC
A technical logTo help you diagnose problemsOn the PC (server-log.txt), replaced each time the app starts

The software doesn't use cameras or microphones, doesn't track location, and doesn't record what anyone sees.

No internet connection is needed

The software works entirely on your local network and makes no connections to the internet. It contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting, and it doesn't check in with any server run by us. The only exceptions are links you choose to click, such as the Full Circle Immersive website or GitHub, which open in your normal web browser.

Who is responsible for the information

Because the information never reaches Full Circle Immersive, we don't hold or process it. If you run sessions for other people, such as students, staff or clients, you or your organisation decide how the software is used, and you're responsible for that use under data protection law, including any recordings of sessions you make yourself.

Deleting information

  • On the PC: delete the Documents\Full Circle Director folder. Uninstalling the app leaves this folder in place, so your videos aren't lost by accident.
  • On a headset: use Remove from headsets in the control panel, or clear the browser's data for the PC's address in the headset's browser settings.

Other companies' services

The software runs inside other companies' products, which have their own privacy policies. The headset page runs in your headset's web browser (for example, the Meta Quest Browser), and if you installed the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect information about installs and app performance under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We don't receive that information.

Children

The software doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children. Organisations using it with young people should follow the headset maker's age guidance and their own safeguarding policies.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over personal information held about you. As Full Circle Immersive doesn't receive or hold any personal information through this software, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For information held on a PC or headset, contact the person or organisation that ran the session.

Changes to this policy

If a future version handles information differently, we'll update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
