# Microsoft Store listing text: Full Circle Director 2.4.0

## Product features (one per box in Partner Center)
1. Control a whole group of Meta Quest headsets from one Windows PC
2. Play 360°, 180° and 3D videos and images in sync on every headset at once
3. Recentre everyone with one click so the whole group faces the same point
4. Laser pointer: point at anything in the scene and every learner sees it
5. Numbered, coloured markers that appear in every headset
6. 360° view on the console: look around the scene just as your learners see it
7. See through any headset's eyes, or watch 1, 4, 9 or 16 headsets at once
8. Show where each person is looking with live field-of-view outlines
9. Send content to the headsets in advance for smooth, full-quality playback
10. Play, pause, skip and stop for everyone; Stop returns all headsets to the waiting screen
11. Live battery and storage levels for every connected headset
12. Works offline on your own Wi-Fi router, with no internet needed
13. No accounts, no cloud: your content stays on your PC and headsets
14. Headsets join through the Quest browser, so there's nothing to install on them
15. Free for personal, education and corporate use
