## Short description
Join a guided 360° session in one tap. Full Circle Player finds Full Circle Director on your Wi-Fi and opens the shared 360° video and images for your group.

## Long description
Full Circle Player is the headset half of Full Circle Director, a free system that lets one person guide a whole room of Meta Quest headsets through 360°, 180° and 3D video and images, all in sync.

Open Full Circle Player and it finds Full Circle Director running on a Windows PC on the same Wi-Fi, then opens the player for you. Nobody has to type an address. If your network blocks automatic discovery, type the address once and the app remembers it.

Once connected, the person running the session can:
• Play, pause and skip for everyone at the same moment
• Recentre the whole group so everyone faces the same point
• Point at anything with a laser pointer that every headset sees
• Place numbered, coloured markers in the scene
• See what each person is looking at, from the PC

Built for teaching, training and shared experiences: clinical skills, moot courts, field trips, site visits and more. It works offline on your own router, with no accounts and no cloud. Your content stays on your PC and your headsets.

Requires Full Circle Director (free, from the Microsoft Store) on a Windows PC on the same Wi-Fi network.

Free for personal, education and corporate use.

## Notes for the reviewer (testing instructions)
Full Circle Player is the headset app for Full Circle Director, a Windows PC application (free on the Microsoft Store) that synchronises 360°/180°/3D video and images across a group of Meta Quest headsets on a local network.

To test:
1. On a Windows PC, install "Full Circle Director" from the Microsoft Store and open it. A sample video can be added with Add files.
2. Put the Quest on the same Wi-Fi network as the PC.
3. Open Full Circle Player on the Quest. It finds the Director within a couple of seconds and opens the player in the Meta Quest Browser.
4. In the browser, accept the one-time certificate notice (the Director runs locally and uses a self-signed certificate), then press Enter VR.
5. On the PC, press Load and Play on any item: the headset plays it. Try the laser pointer (L), markers (M) and Recentre everyone.

If automatic discovery is blocked on your network, type the IP address shown at the top of the Director window into the app and press Open.
A video of a full session is available on request.
